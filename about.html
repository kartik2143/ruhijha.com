About

Where finance meets nature.

Profile

I work in nature and sustainable finance in Paris. At AXA Climate I focus on nature-based solutions — part of a broader aim to shift the financial sector toward a nature-positive economy.

My work spans both sides of that question. With the UN Environment Programme I built nature–climate fiscal alignment tools and biodiversity-linked budget tagging for ministries of finance; at Amundi I analysed ESG risk across institutional portfolios under the EU's sustainable-finance framework; and through ESG advisory I have helped structure climate-aligned infrastructure finance. The thread runs back further still — to early research on the links between environment, sustainability and financial performance.

I hold degrees from Sciences Po and the University of California, Berkeley, and work in English and French.
